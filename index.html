About Who We Are

Wedding Cinematography & Storytelling

Phones just don't feel this way. We believe in emotion, in timelessness, and in capturing moments that matter.

Welcome to Keepsake — a team of wedding content creators from Sri Lanka. Think beyond traditional photo and video: we craft candid, cinematic content made for both keepsakes and sharing in the moment. Unposed and unscripted, our reels, short films, and imagery capture the laughter, the fleeting glances, and the real atmosphere of your day — delivered swiftly, so your memories feel as alive as when they happened.
